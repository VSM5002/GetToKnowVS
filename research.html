IEEE CONFERENCE PUBLICATION · 2025

AI-Based Resource Allocation in 5G Networks for Urban Events: Challenges and Solutions

2025 International Conference on Communication, Computer, and Information Technology (IC3IT)

About the Research

This research explores a machine learning-based approach to improving resource allocation in 5G networks during high-demand urban events.

The work focuses on predicting network requirements and allocating resources to improve throughput while reducing latency and packet loss.

Methodology

Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) models were used to study network resource allocation and evaluate network performance.
